## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I customize keybinds or use defaults?**

Customize keybinds to match your reflexes. Defaults are starting points.

**How long to adjust to new keybinds?**

Approximately 1 — 2 weeks for major changes. Minor changes take less time.

**What is the most important keybind to optimize?**

Flashlight is the most important because it is the most-used secondary action.

**Should I use mouse buttons for actions?**

Yes, mouse buttons provide quick access to frequently used actions like flashlight and sprint.

**How often should I re-optimize keybinds?**

After major game updates or when you notice specific inefficiencies.

For more on the platform-specific controls, see our [PC controls guide](/en/controls/pc-controls/). To learn the broader controls reference, read [full controls list](/en/controls/full-controls-list/).


**What is the best controller layout for left-handed players?**

Left-handed players can customize button layouts through Roblox accessibility settings. Many players remap movement to the right stick and camera to the left stick for left-hand primary control. Some controllers offer physical left-handed configurations. Experiment to find what works for your specific needs.

**How do I improve my reaction time for flashlight clicks?**

Reaction time improves with deliberate practice. Start by clicking at predictable intervals (every 5 seconds), then randomize the timing. Use online reaction time trainers to improve baseline reflexes. In-game practice with audio cues builds game-specific timing. Most players see significant improvement within 2 — 4 weeks of focused practice.

**Can I use eye tracking or accessibility hardware?**

Standard Roblox does not support specialized accessibility hardware, but some third-party tools may work. Voice control through Windows Speech Recognition or similar tools can simulate basic controls. For serious accessibility needs, contact Roblox support for current capabilities.

**What is the best DPI for horror games?**

DPI between 800 and 1600 is standard for horror games. Higher DPI allows finer movements but requires careful in-game sensitivity adjustment. Most professional players use 800 — 1200 DPI paired with moderate in-game sensitivity. Test different combinations to find what feels comfortable for your reflexes.

**How do I deal with input lag on Roblox?**

Input lag can be caused by network issues, system performance, or Roblox platform problems. Check your internet connection stability first. Close background applications that may consume resources. Lower Roblox graphics settings if performance is unstable. Contact Roblox support for platform-specific issues that persist.

**Should I learn multiple input methods?**

Learning both mouse and gamepad provides flexibility. Mouse is optimal for precision clicking, while gamepad offers comfort. Mobile players should learn touch controls as a baseline, with optional external controller use for serious play. Versatility helps in various server configurations.

**How do I customize controls for streaming?**

Streaming setups often require audio routing adjustments. Separate game audio from microphone input to prevent echo. Use voice chat configurations that allow clean recording. Many streamers use multiple audio devices to separate in-game audio, voice chat, and microphone for recording flexibility.


**What is the most common mistake when starting out?**

The most common mistake is treating Clark Huss rooms like a typical horror game where visual cues dominate. Audio-first design means new players must develop listening skills that other games do not require. This learning curve catches many experienced horror game players who rely on visual instincts they have built elsewhere.

**How do experienced players differ from beginners?**

Experienced players move more deliberately, use flashlight less frequently, communicate more clearly in squads, and recognize Clark audio tells within fractions of a second. Beginners tend to sprint, hold flashlight continuously, panic during chases, and miss critical audio cues. The gap closes with deliberate practice over 5 — 10 sessions.

**What is the biggest improvement you can make quickly?**

Improving flashlight discipline provides the fastest improvement for most players. Switching from continuous flashlight use to click-only mode extends battery life dramatically. The extended battery life means more visual information is available when needed, improving overall performance.

**How important is audio equipment really?**

Audio equipment is critical. Built-in laptop speakers and TV speakers cannot provide the directional audio cues the game is designed around. A $30 pair of wired earbuds dramatically improves gameplay compared to built-in speakers. The investment pays off immediately.

**Should I focus on solo or squad play?**

Focus on the mode that matches your goals. Solo play builds individual skills faster. Squad play provides longer survival times and social engagement. Most veterans play both modes to develop well-rounded abilities. Choose based on your priorities and available time.

**What role does luck play in survival?**

Luck affects run outcomes significantly because of procedural generation. A well-prepared player in an unlucky layout may die, while a less prepared player in a favorable layout may survive. Focus on skill development to minimize the role of luck over time. Long-term success requires consistency across many runs.

**How do I avoid burnout?**

Burnout is real for active players. Take breaks between sessions, play other games, and vary your squadmates. Set realistic goals for each session. If you find yourself frustrated, step away for a day or two. Returning fresh improves both enjoyment and performance.

**What is the best way to learn advanced techniques?**

Study veteran gameplay through community resources. Watch experienced players on streaming platforms. Read detailed guides on specific topics. Practice specific techniques in isolation before applying them in real runs. Deliberate learning accelerates skill development compared to passive gameplay.
