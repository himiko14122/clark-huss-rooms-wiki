## How Clark's Audio Tracking Works

Clark's audio tracking is a layered system that combines distance, sound type, and player behavior to determine detection. The system runs continuously during a run.

### **Audio tracking pipeline:**

| Stage | Process | Output |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Capture audio sources | Raw sound data |
| 2 | Classify sound types | Sprint/click/voice/etc. |
| 3 | Calculate distance | Volume-distance curve |
| 4 | Apply modifiers | Sensitivity boosts |
| 5 | Determine state transition | Escalation decision |
| 6 | Update tracking | Path to target |

The pipeline runs every frame, meaning Clark's detection is real-time. There is no delay between sound and detection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How accurate is Clark's audio tracking?**

Clark's tracking is very accurate for sounds within detection range. Outside detection range, sounds are ignored. The accuracy makes sound discipline critical.

**Can Clark hear through walls?**

Partially. Sound can penetrate walls at reduced volume. Clark's detection range accounts for this penetration.

**What is the quietest sound Clark detects?**

Walking footsteps are the quietest meaningful sound. Breathing is quieter but shorter range.

**How do I know if Clark is tracking me?**

Heartbeat acceleration, breathing cadence, and whisper fragments all indicate increasing tracking. If you hear these, Clark is aware of your position.

**Can I confuse Clark with multiple sounds?**

Yes, in squad play. Solo players have limited ability to confuse Clark because they have only one audio source.
