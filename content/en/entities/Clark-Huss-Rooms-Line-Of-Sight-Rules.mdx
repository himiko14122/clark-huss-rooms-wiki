## What Line Of Sight Means In Clark Huss rooms

Line of sight (LOS) refers to Clark's ability to see your character model through the procedurally generated corridor geometry. When LOS is established, Clark's tracking accuracy increases dramatically.

### **Line of sight states:**

| State | Clark Tracking | Counter-Measure |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| Direct LOS | High accuracy | Break LOS immediately |
| LOS through thin wall | Reduced accuracy | Move to break |
| LOS through thick wall | Audio only | Audio masking |
| LOS broken | Audio only | Audio masking |
| No LOS possible | Audio only | Standard navigation |

The state changes dynamically as you and Clark move through the corridors.

## Wall Thickness Mechanics

### **Wall thickness effects:**

| Thickness | Light Penetration | Visual LOS |

The lighting rules are why flashlight use can reveal your position through thin walls but not thick ones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How effective is corner break against active chase?**

Single corner break has roughly 50% success rate. Two consecutive breaks have 80%+ success rate. Three breaks are near-certain.

**Can Clark see through multiple walls?**

No. Clark's visual detection stops at two wall layers. Audio continues but visual is blocked.

**Does my own flashlight affect my LoS visibility?**

Yes. Your flashlight illuminates walls and can reveal your silhouette through thin walls. Click discipline reduces this effect.

**What is the best room geometry for escape?**

L-shaped corridors with multiple turns are best. Open rooms are worst because they provide full 360-degree visibility.

**Does hiding behind decoration break LoS?**

Yes, decoration objects block LoS. However, Clark may path around them to re-establish visual contact.
