Clark is the only confirmed entity in Clark Huss rooms as of July 2026, but his threat profile is multi-layered because his behavior shifts based on awareness, distance, and squad configuration. Understanding Clark's threat tier under each condition lets you prioritize survival decisions correctly. New players treat Clark as a binary threat (alive or dead), which causes them to either overreact to distant heartbeats or underreact to close whispers.

This tier list ranks Clark's threat under each condition, explains the behavioral shifts that trigger tier changes, and provides counter-strategy recommendations for each tier. You will also learn how the tier list evolves as more entities are added in future Early Access updates.

## Threat Tier Framework

Clark's threat is rated on a six-tier scale that ranges from inert to imminent. Each tier has specific audio cues, detection ranges, and counter-strategy requirements.

### **Threat tier definitions:**

| Tier | Label | Clark State | Detection Range | Counter-Strategy |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------------|------------------|
| S | Imminent | Active chase, line of sight | 20+ meters | Full sprint, flashlight off, corner break |
| A | High | Whispering, tracking your trail | 12 — 18 meters | Controlled retreat, audio monitoring |
| B | Medium | Breathing cadence, one corridor | 6 — 12 meters | Pause, identify direction, plan escape |
| C | Low | Footstep thuds, adjacent room | 3 — 6 meters | Slow movement, listen carefully |
| D | Distant | Heartbeat only, two corridors | 1 — 3 meters | Normal pace, flashlight clicks allowed |
| F | Inert | Out of range or cooldown | 0 meters | Free flashlight use, click frequently |

The tier framework lets you classify Clark's threat within one second of hearing any audio cue. Veterans classify automatically without conscious thought.

## Tier S: Imminent Threat

Tier S is the highest threat level and occurs when Clark is in active chase with potential line of sight. Survival requires immediate, decisive action.

### **Tier S triggers:**

- Sprint burst audio tell
- Whisper fragments combined with footstep thuds
- Visual confirmation of Clark approaching
- Squadmate callout "Chase, full sprint now"

### **Tier S response protocol:**

| Step | Action | Time Required |
|------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | Full sprint in chosen direction | 0 — 1 sec |
| 2 | Flashlight off | 0.5 sec |
| 3 | Corner break at nearest junction | 1 — 3 sec |
| 4 | Second corner break | 3 — 6 sec |
| 5 | Verify Clark disengagement | 6 — 10 sec |

The Tier S protocol takes ten seconds to execute and requires full stamina reserves. If you enter Tier S with low stamina, your survival probability drops by 60%.

## Tier A: High Threat

Tier A is the second-highest threat and occurs when Clark is actively tracking your audio trail but has not yet engaged in chase. This tier gives you a ten to fifteen second window to break the trail.

### **Tier A triggers:**

- Repeated whisper fragments
- Rapid breathing cadence
- Heartbeat accelerating to 100+ BPM
- Clark vocalization (single-word mutter)

**Tier A counter-strategies:**

1. Stop sprinting immediately — sprint audio is what he is tracking
2. Switch to walking pace only
3. Take a sharp corner break to disrupt audio trail
4. Click flashlight once to verify Clark position
5. Wait fifteen seconds for Clark to disengage

The Tier A counter-strategy is patience. New players panic and sprint, which guarantees tier escalation to S. Veterans wait and let Clark lose the audio trail naturally.

## Tier B: Medium Threat

Tier B is the most common threat level during mid-game and occurs when Clark is within audio detection range but has not identified your specific position. This tier is the sweet spot for survival decision-making.

**Tier B triggers:**

- Normal breathing cadence (one breath per 2 seconds)
- Footstep thuds at slow tempo
- Heartbeat in normal range (60 — 80 BPM)
- Distant whisper fragments

**Tier B decision framework:**

| Clark Position | Recommended Action |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Behind squad | Continue forward, slight pace increase |
| Ahead of squad | Pause, identify escape routes |
| To the left | Move toward right corridor |
| To the right | Move toward left corridor |
| Same corridor | Controlled retreat |

Tier B is where most players die because they underestimate the threat. The breathing cadence sounds manageable, but Clark is actively listening and can escalate to Tier A within seconds.

## Tier C: Low Threat

Tier C is the routine navigation tier where Clark is nearby but not actively threatening. Most of your run time is spent in Tier C, which makes it the most important to optimize.

**Tier C characteristics:**

- Slow footstep thuds (two per second)
- Slow breathing cadence (one breath per 4 seconds)
- Distant heartbeat (50 — 60 BPM)
- No whisper fragments

**Tier C flashlight policy:**

| Battery State | Flashlight Behavior |
|---------------|---------------------|
| 100 — 70% | Normal click pattern |
| 70 — 50% | Reduce to single clicks |
| 50 — 25% | Click only at decision points |
| Below 25% | Audio-only navigation |

Tier C is also where most of your spatial mapping happens. Build your mental map during Tier C because Tier A and S do not allow time for mapping.

## Tier D: Distant Threat

Tier D is the safest non-inert tier and represents Clark patrolling in a distant area of the server. Most of the early game is spent in Tier D.

**Tier D characteristics:**

- Heartbeat audible but low volume
- No breathing or footstep cues
- No whisper fragments
- Stable pulse rhythm

**Tier D opportunity window:**

Tier D provides the best window for flashlight exploration and spatial mapping. The flashlight policy in Tier D allows for more clicks because Clark's detection range is minimal.

| Activity | Tier D Recommendation |
|----------|----------------------|
| Flashlight clicking | High frequency, up to 10 clicks per minute |
| Spatial mapping | Full corridor exploration |
| Squad regrouping | Pause and plan |
| Item collection | Thorough search |
| Stamina recovery | Full rest |

Veteran players use Tier D windows to prepare for the next tier escalation. The preparation makes the difference between surviving Tier B and dying in Tier A.

## Tier F: Inert State

Tier F represents the inert state when Clark is either out of audio range, in a cooldown window, or in a server state that prevents detection. Tier F is the safest time for aggressive flashlight use and aggressive navigation.

**Tier F triggers:**

- Clark's heartbeat is inaudible for 30+ seconds
- Squad has reached a marked safe corridor
- Clark is in active cooldown after disengagement
- Server state prevents detection (very rare)

**Tier F flashlight policy:**

In Tier F, flashlight use is unrestricted. Click as frequently as needed for navigation. This is the window to gather information about corridor layout, item locations, and exit indicators.
